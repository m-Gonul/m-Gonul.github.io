Synthetic facial palsy from healthy faces

TÜBİTAK 2209-A undergraduate research project, ongoing

  • Built a parsing-space synthesis pipeline: side-specific displacement fields warp semantic segmentation maps, which generative models then re-render. Twenty scenarios defined with clinical experts on 29,928 CelebAMask-HQ faces.
  • Benchmarked four open vision-language models (Gemma, MedGemma, Qwen2.5-VL, Qwen3-VL) as realism and asymmetry judges, filtered the set into strict (4,419) and broad (11,022) tiers, and designed a 500-image human realism study.
  • Fine-tuned EVA-02, ConvNeXt, ViT and CAFormer backbones on H100 GPUs with identity-disjoint splits and patient-level 5-fold cross-validation on 88 real patients.
